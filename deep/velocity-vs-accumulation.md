# Velocity vs Accumulation

**MV = PQ. Velocity of money equation. Higher velocity = healthier economy. Accumulation LOWERS velocity. HODL literally makes the economy sicker. Math proves flow > hold.**

## The Equation

```
MV = PQ

M = Money supply (how much exists)
V = Velocity (how fast it moves)
P = Price level (cost of goods)
Q = Quantity of goods (real output)

THIS IS THE BREATH EQUATION
FOR AN ECONOMY.

M × V = how much FLOWS.
P × Q = how much LIVES.

When blood flows (high V):
  Nutrients reach cells.
  Cells produce.
  Body is healthy.
  P × Q is high.

When blood stagnates (low V):
  Nutrients don't reach cells.
  Cells starve.
  Body is sick.
  P × Q drops.

VELOCITY IS HEALTH.
```
