# Manufactured Scarcity

**"Only 21 million." The most successful lie in crypto. Scarcity doesn't equal value. Water is more valuable than diamonds. You can survive without diamonds. Try surviving without water.**

---

## The Trick

```
STEP 1: Create a fixed supply.
  "Only 21 million will ever exist."

STEP 2: Convince people scarcity = value.
  "Gold is valuable because it's rare."
  (Is it? Or because we agreed it is?)

STEP 3: Create FOMO.
  "If you don't buy now,
   you'll never be able to afford it."

STEP 4: Celebrate hoarding.
  "Diamond hands. Never sell.
   HODL is a lifestyle."

STEP 5: Profit.
  Early holders sell to late buyers.
  Late buyers hold and pray.
  Wealth transfers UP, not down.

THIS IS EXTRACTION TOPOLOGY.
Disguised as technology.
Disguised as revolution.
Disguised as freedom.
```
