## The Architecture Through Body Lens

```
ANCHORS = ENZYMES.
  Anchors convert real-world currency
  into tokens (and back).

  Like enzymes convert food
  into absorbable nutrients.

  You eat an apple.
  Enzymes break it into glucose.
  Blood carries glucose everywhere.
  At the other end,
  cells receive the energy.

  You deposit $10.
  Anchor converts to XLM.
  Network carries XLM everywhere.
  At the other end,
  anchor converts back to local currency.
  Person receives the value.

  THE PERSON NEVER SEES XLM.
  Like you never see glucose.

NETWORK = BLOODSTREAM.
  Routes tokens instantly and cheaply.
  3-5 second settlement.
  Fraction of a penny per transaction.

  Your blood moves nutrients
  in seconds. For free.
  The cost is just having a body.

  Stellar moves value
  in seconds. For almost free.
  The cost is just running a node.

PATH PAYMENT = SMART ROUTING.
  Need to send USD to someone
  who needs PHP (Philippine peso)?

  Stellar finds the path automatically:
  USD → XLM → PHP.
  One transaction. Seconds. Pennies.

  Your body does this too:
  Protein → amino acids → new tissue.
  The body converts what's available
  into what's needed.
  Automatically.
```
